# Remote Work Policy

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Employees in customer-facing support roles must remain on-site during core business hours (9am-3pm local time) regardless of tenure.

## Equipment

The company will provide a laptop and a one-time $500 home office stipend for employees approved for remote work.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable via Slack and email between 10am and 4pm in their local time zone.
