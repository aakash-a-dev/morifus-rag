# Employee Handbook — Section 4: Workplace Flexibility

**Document owner:** HR Leadership
**Effective date:** March 15, 2025

## Remote Work

All employees, regardless of role or department, are permitted to work fully remote on a permanent basis. There is no in-office attendance requirement and no minimum tenure requirement to qualify for remote work arrangements.

## Availability

Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings but are not required to maintain fixed daily availability windows.
