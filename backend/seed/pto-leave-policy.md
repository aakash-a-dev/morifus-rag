# PTO & Leave Policy

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025

## Annual PTO Allowance

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to 10 company-observed holidays. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 8 separate paid sick days per year, which do not count against the PTO allowance.

## Requesting Time Off

PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the HR portal.
