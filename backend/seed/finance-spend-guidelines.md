# Finance Spend Guidelines (Regional Addendum)

**Document owner:** Finance — APAC Region
**Effective date:** February 1, 2025

## Self-Approval Limit

Employees may self-approve and submit expenses up to $75 without manager sign-off. Any expense exceeding $75 requires manager approval before submission, and amounts over $2,000 require Finance director sign-off.

## Meal Allowance

Meals during business travel are capped at $45 per day, inclusive of all meals.
