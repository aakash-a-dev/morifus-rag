CODE OF CONDUCT
Document owner: Legal & Compliance
Effective date: January 1, 2025

1. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism at all times.

2. TIME OFF ENTITLEMENT
For clarity, employees are entitled to 22 days of paid vacation annually, plus applicable public holidays, as the company's standard leave entitlement.

3. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any outside employment or financial interest that could reasonably be seen to conflict with the company's business interests.

4. REPORTING CONCERNS
Concerns about workplace conduct should be reported to HR or via the anonymous ethics hotline.
