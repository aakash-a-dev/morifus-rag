# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document owner:** Finance
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025

## Approval Thresholds

Expenses under $150 may be self-approved by the employee and submitted directly for reimbursement. Expenses between $150 and $1,000 require manager approval. Expenses above $1,000 require both manager and Finance director approval.

## Travel

Domestic travel expenses (flights, hotels) must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to $60 per day.

## Submission Window

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
